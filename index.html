01 / FORUM

The forum at a glance

More than a conference. A working ecosystem for two days.

Future Food Forum 2026 brings together government, industry, investors, innovators and food-sector leaders to accelerate a more resilient, sustainable and inclusive food future.

Future Food Forum 2026 is the UAE's annual strategic convergence platform, bringing together government, industry, academia, investors, innovators and food-sector leaders to review progress, align priorities and shape a more resilient, sustainable and inclusive food future.

“From Resilience to Recovery” Under this theme, the Forum will convene leaders around the priorities shaping the food ecosystem: food security, trade, innovation, sustainability, technology and investment.
